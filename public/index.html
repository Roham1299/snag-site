A fast download manager that lives in your browser.

Hover over a video and a Download button appears on it. Pick a quality and Snag saves it, properly named, at full speed. Whole playlists and seasons too, and every big download from your browser.

Download Snag See what it does

Free to use, for Windows, macOS and Linux.

A button on every video

Every quality from 4K down, audio only, subtitles in your language, lyrics for songs, and clips cut to the exact frame. Works on about 1,800 video sites and on any page that plays a video file or stream.

Full speed, and it resumes

Several connections per file, re-split as parts finish. Pause and resume any time, even after a restart or a dropped connection.

Your browser's downloads, handed over

Archives, programs, videos and documents go to Snag automatically. If Snag can't take one, the browser gets it back. Hold Delete while you click to keep one in the browser.

Many links at once

Right-click a page to grab its links, or paste a list. Snag sorts them, finds their real names and sizes, and downloads the ones you tick.

Subscriptions

Follow a channel, a playlist, a podcast or your Spotify Liked Songs. Snag checks for new ones every few hours and downloads them the way you chose. Part of Snag Cloud, with a free trial.

Checksums

When the page or the server says what a file's checksum should be, Snag offers to check it, and to download it again if it doesn't match.

Playlists and seasons, in one go

Open a playlist, a channel, an album or a season, tick what you want, and choose the quality, the folder and how files are named. Episodes are saved as Show S01E03 - Title, in their own queue.

Tools for the files you already have

Point Snag at a folder and it says what could be better, from videos without subtitles to copies you don't need. Every tool is free, runs on your computer, and changes nothing until you press its button.

Subtitles & lyrics

Subtitles from OpenSubtitles, with your own free key, or from the site the episodes came from. Lyrics from LRCLIB, timed line by line.

Tags & covers

Titles, artists, albums and 1200-pixel covers from Deezer and Apple Music, episode titles from TVmaze. Pick another match, or search, when it isn't sure.

Rename

Names a folder's episodes, songs or videos one way, with episode titles from TVmaze. Subtitle files follow, and the last rename can be undone.

Convert & shrink

Makes videos play anywhere, fit under a size for chat apps, or take about half the space, with the encoders your computer has. Or keeps just the sound.

Find duplicates

Finds a song or video kept more than once, by its sound, name or bytes. It keeps the original and moves the extras to the Recycle Bin or Trash.

Check files

Finds files that won't open, stop early, or came out smaller than they downloaded, and gets Snag's own downloads again.

Private by design

Snag runs entirely on your computer, and the extension talks only to the Snag app on the same machine. Every hour the app reads a small signed file from this website to learn of new releases. To find lyrics, tags, covers or subtitles, it asks LRCLIB, Deezer, Apple Music, TVmaze or OpenSubtitles about that song or show. It sends nothing else about you or your downloads.
